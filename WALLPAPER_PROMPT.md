# Billighost OS — Wallpaper Generation Prompt

Use this prompt in ChatGPT (GPT-4o / DALL·E 3) or Midjourney to generate a stunning custom wallpaper for Billighost OS.

---

### 🎨 The Prompt (Copy & Paste)

```text
A minimal, ultra-clean desktop wallpaper for a futuristic, dark-mode operating system named Billighost OS. Deep obsidian void background (#050505 to #0c182a) with organic, fluid ribbon waves of translucent frosted glass illuminated from within. The glowing currents gently transition from ethereal spectral cyan-blue (#7eb8ff) into soft luminous lavender-violet (#a78bfa). Subtle volumetric caustics, smooth specular reflections, microscopic cosmic stardust, and soft gradients. Cinematic lighting, raytraced glass refraction, sleek modern digital art, 8k resolution, elegant, spacious, noise-free, zero clutter, no text, no logos, horizontal 16:9 desktop aspect ratio.
```

---

### 📁 Where to Put Your Wallpaper

Once ChatGPT generates your wallpaper, save it and place it in either of these locations:

1. **`public/wallpaper.png`** (Recommended — immediately available)
   *or*
2. **`src/assets/wallpaper.png`**

> **Note:** Both `.png` and `.jpg` are supported. Billighost OS will automatically detect and apply it across the Lock Screen, PIN Screen, and Desktop Screen!

---

### 👤 Profile Picture / Avatar

You can also place your own profile picture at:
- **`src/assets/avatar.png`**

If not present, Billighost OS displays an elegant glowing initial badge.
